The linear Kalman filter is widely used in magnetic and inertial measurement unit-based attitude estimation, but its performance relies on accurate estimation of the model error covariance matrix of the measurement equation. Existing methods either adopt fixed empirical values with poor adaptability or complex analytical models with high computational cost. This study proposes a least squares regression model, expressed as P(i,j)=ALX(qmqn) : here, P(i,j) denotes the element at the i-th row and j-th column of the covariance matrix P, X(qmqn) is a vector formed by stacking products of any two elements of the quaternion q, and non-zero elements of matrix AL are polynomial functions of geomagnetic field inclination and measurement error standard deviations of accelerometer and magnetometer. Experiments including simulations, static tests with ADIS16480, dynamic tests with MTi and with a public dataset show the model matches the accuracy of typical analytical methods (such as Quaternion Estimator, Algebraic Quaternion Algorithm, and Novel Quaternion Determination Method), has lower computation time, avoids the singularity issue of Quaternion Estimator, and enhances the robustness of the linear Kalman filter.
This repository provides all source codes and data of this study, enabling researchers to promote and apply the research outcomes, as well as further refine the study based on this foundation.
In this repository, the Quaternion Estimator, Algebraic Quaternion Algorithm, and Novel Quaternion Determination Method are named QUEST3, AQUA3, and NQDM3 respectively. LKF3 is the source code for the Linear Kalman Filter (LKF), and the above three programs are uniformly called by AttitudeCalBatchData3. SimulationData3 is used to generate simulation data; FindRelationQuaternion_P3 is for obtaining the specific values of elements in the regression matrix AL; CoefStd is for acquiring the specific coefficients of the polynomial model that describes the mathematical relationship between the regression matrix AL and three parameters (i.e., geomagnetic field inclination, and measurement error standard deviations of the accelerometer and magnetometer). ADIS16480StaticData3 provides the output results of ADIS16480 at multiple static positions, while XSensMTiDynamicData3 and EMSDynamicData3 offer the dynamic output results from MTi and the attitude reference from the electromagnetic tracking system, respectively.
